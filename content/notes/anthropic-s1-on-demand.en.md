> For the first time in history, intelligence is something humans can build. The cognitive capability to reason, analyze, synthesize, decide, and create is producible at scale and accessible on demand.

In the prospectus: [Prospectus summary: Overview](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm#ica694c859b8e458dbf6a73f56dde9d42_9895604651019:~:text=For%20the%20first%20time%20in%20history,accessible%20on%20demand) ([original filing on SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm))

The words “accessible on demand” caught my eye right away, and they immediately made me think of how ordinary workers are hired by the month, not on demand. In some situations that is a huge advantage: a legal adviser handling a case, an accountant doing quarterly reports, a translator used as needed, and so on. Many tasks don’t need continuous employment; they only need someone to step in for a while, do the whole job, and then be done.
