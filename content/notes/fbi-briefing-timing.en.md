Reports say the FBI knew about Jason and Huang Le a year ago and presented a briefing on them to the President. The information may well all be true, but what is odd is the timing: why does it have to be made public now? It comes just as the midterm elections have been settled and the Trump administration's power is weakening, so it is hard not to suspect that the decision to release it was made under the Democrats' influence.
