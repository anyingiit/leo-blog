> **Ankur Moitra**：我还觉得，学生们对发表论文的前景太焦虑了，以至于最难的事情之一是：我构思一个项目时，其实是在用好几年的时间跨度去考虑它，因为我心里有一个想要实现的更大的愿景，比如：怎样把学习理论的技术用到量子多体系统上？通往那里的路上有很多垫脚石，而一些最激动人心的问题，还在很多年之后。可是学生们太担心即将到来的AI浪潮了——工作的未来、全民基本收入、我们还会有工作吗？于是他们真正考虑的是三个月、六个月的时间跨度。这样一来，就很难找到我们都关心的问题，因为……你知道，我们想的是两个不同的世界。
>
> **Lex Fridman**：你不觉得我们应该鼓励他们继续做长远的思考吗？我是说，我理解……
>
> **Ankur Moitra**：哦，我当然这么想，但他们不一定听你的。我是说，我不知道自己是对是错，也许AI会解决我们所有的问题，但看起来，此时此刻真正值得我们去做的，只有那些再过好些年我们仍然会关心的问题，你只应该去做经得起未来考验的问题。如果你做的事情让你很着急，因为你觉得下一代模型肯定会解决这些问题，那也许干脆去度个假，别去解决那个问题了。

英文原文：

> **Ankur Moitra**: I think also students have so much anxiety about, you know, what publications are gonna look like that one of the hardest parts is, you know, when I'm thinking about a project, I'm really trying to think about it in a sort of multi-year time horizon because I have some meta vision that I wanna realize, like how can we use learning theory techniques in quantum many-body systems? And there are a lot of stepping stones on route to that, but some of the most exciting problems are, you know, many years out. But students are so worried about the incoming AI tidal wave, you know, the future of work, universal basic income. Are any of us gonna have jobs? That they're really thinking in three- and six-month horizons, and then it's hard to find problems that we both care about just because- You know, we're thinking about different worlds.
>
> **Lex Fridman**: You don't think we should encourage them to continue to think long term? I mean, I understand that-
>
> **Ankur Moitra**: Oh, I do, but they don't always listen to you. I mean, I don't know if I'm right or wrong. You know, maybe AI will solve all of our problems, but it seems like the only ones that are actually worth us working on at this moment in time are the ones we're still gonna care about in a bunch of years, and you should only be working on future-proof problems. If you're working on things that, you know, you're in a rush because you think the next generation of models will definitely solve those problems, then maybe just go on vacation and don't solve that problem.
