> #### AI is Transforming Intelligence
>
> We believe that Powerful AI, a highly capable and impactful form of frontier AI characterized by the following key attributes, will become widely available within a few years:
>
> - cognitive capabilities matching or exceeding those of top human experts across most professional and scientific disciplines;
>
> - the ability to navigate all interfaces available to a human working virtually, including text, audio, video, and direct computer control;
>
> - the ability to reason through complex tasks autonomously over extended periods—lasting hours, days, or weeks—and seek clarification and feedback when needed;
>
> - the ability to coordinate across multiple instances and collaborate with both humans and other AI systems; and
>
> - the ability to interface with the physical world by digitally controlling laboratory equipment, robotic systems, and other machines through digital connections.
>
> If Powerful AI is directed at solving humanity’s hardest problems and the risks are managed responsibly, the potential is extraordinary. The possibilities include compressing decades of scientific progress into years; accelerating drug discovery, disease detection, and the delivery of personalized therapy; providing individually tailored education and expert financial and legal advice; advancing power grids and battery design for greater efficiency and reliability; and extending to the developing world capabilities that took wealthy nations generations to build.

I broadly agree, especially with the line “cognitive capabilities matching or exceeding those of top human experts across most professional and scientific disciplines.” If AI can solve math problems (a while ago I saw that Navier–Stokes, one of the Millennium Prize Problems, had been solved), then what professional problem is there that AI can’t solve?

Mathematics has always been seen as the hardest kind of problem human intelligence can solve. If even this can be solved, what reason is there that other problems that depend on intelligence can’t be?
