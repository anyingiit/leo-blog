> Enterprise Strategy. Our enterprise strategy is built on the belief that frontier AI is most valuable in real-world settings, as deployment across enterprises is where economic impact is greatest and where the improvement loops from customer feedback are richest. Accordingly, we have organized our research priorities, our product roadmap, and our go-to-market and Applied AI teams around enterprise use cases, which range from routine knowledge work to the most complex problems. The customer feedback we gather directs our research focus and makes Claude more capable over time. This broadens where Claude can be deployed and further expands enterprise adoption, which helps ensure that the benefits of our AI reach people and organizations at scale.
